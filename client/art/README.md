# Realms front-page artwork

`realms-dawn.webp` is original concept artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then resized to 1280 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at quality 78 without cropping or altering the composition. It is atmosphere for the public welcome screen, not a gameplay screenshot or a promise of specific playable locations. The browser loads it from the same origin through the explicit client-asset allowlist.

## Generation prompt

Use case: illustration. Asset type: wide landscape hero artwork for the browser fantasy role-playing game Realms. Create an original painterly fantasy landscape, no text, no logos, no UI. A quiet winding stone road through a lush ancient forest leads to a distant small medieval citadel and watchtower on a rocky ridge, waterfalls and misty blue-green mountains behind, soft golden dawn light in the sky. A tiny cloaked traveler seen from behind on the path suggests an invitation to adventure, not combat. Elegant hand-painted book-cover illustration, detailed natural foliage and weathered architecture, atmospheric depth, sophisticated earthy evergreen, sage, ivory and antique-gold palette, luminous but restrained. Wide 3:2 composition suitable for a panoramic crop, keep the road, citadel and strongest focal details toward the center and right; upper left is calmer mist and trees. No weapons closeups, no identifiable existing fantasy franchises, no typography, no border, no watermark. This is aspirational visual atmosphere, not a screenshot of gameplay.

## Tavern cover artwork

`realms-tavern.webp` is the current cover artwork, generated with the built-in imagegen tool and resized to 1600 × 533 pixels, encoded as WebP at quality 82. It is original tabletop/tavern concept atmosphere, not a gameplay screenshot. The previous landscape remains available as an archived asset.

### Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: panoramic cover banner for the fantasy browser RPG Realms. Primary request: an original cozy medieval tavern scene centered on tabletop role-playing. A worn oak gaming table with a hand-drawn parchment adventure map, several colorful jewel-toned polyhedral dice, a small pewter adventurer miniature, leather-bound game journal, a ceramic tankard, and a candle. Background: warm timber beams, softly glowing hearth and blurred tavern shelves. Painterly fantasy book illustration, tactile wood, paper and brass, sophisticated warm amber light, moss green shadows, burgundy and teal dice. Wide panoramic composition around 3:1; main tabletop objects clearly visible at center-right within the middle horizontal band so they survive a short website banner crop. Left third quieter and darker for an overlaid title that will be added in HTML. Eye level angled down slightly at the gaming table. Welcoming, intimate, richly crafted but uncluttered. No people needed. No text, logos, watermark, UI, borders, branded game products or recognizable franchise characters. This is concept atmosphere, not a gameplay screenshot.
