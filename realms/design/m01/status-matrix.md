## Scope and missing work

- **Core — Core Design Bible**: Broad intent retained; map obligations to owning systems.
- **01 — Turns, Rollover & Daily Economy**: Planner, banked charges, reserves, rests and pinned rollover in fixtures; production policy and Home integrations open.
- **02 — Food, Booze, Tolerance & Consumables**: Consumption, nightcap guards, onboarding undo and meal-plan UI; no production food catalog, restaurant/feast workflow or complete Studio editor.
- **03 — Character Progression, Attributes, Classes & Mastery**: XP, build choices and advancement history; training, respec, bounded Mastery and complete class engines open.
- **04 — Tactical Combat, Actions, Positioning, Damage & Conditions**: Saved solo/shared combat, battlefield, effects, resources and recap; general reactions, setup phase, production encounter balance and catalogs open.
- **05 — Encounters, Manipulation, Wanderers & Zone State**: Committed encounter draws; full zone state, manipulation queues and wilderness loop open.
- **06 — Quests, Journal, Rumors, Contracts, Bounties & Consequences**: Shared checks are adapters, not a quest graph, Journal progression, fail-forward or principal reward owner.
- **07 — Items, Equipment, Inventory, Rarity, Quality & Durability**: Inventory, equipment, protection, storage, carrying, repair and browsing; complete durability, modification and authored catalog open.
- **08 — Crafting, Gathering, Professions, Quality & Maker Economy**: Routine recipes conserve inputs/outputs; gathering, stations, professions, quality and commissions open.
- **09 — Familiars, Companions, Hirelings, Summons & Party AI**: Owned tactical companion recruitment and shared control; full companion/Familiar progression, personality and automation open.
- **10 — Ascension, Run Modes, Legacy, DM Tavern & Run History**: Fixture victory, preview, reset, persistent discoveries and new build; full Tavern, modes, Legacy/Mastery and Chronicles open.
- **11 — Economy, Markets, Trade, Gold, Services & Inflation Control**: Conserved Gold ledger and restricted transfers/services; complete vendor, trade, escrow and market player flows open.
- **12 — Guilds, Social Systems, Guild Halls, Campaigns & Community**: No integrated Guild player workflow established.
- **13 — Aftercore, Endgame, Raids, Expeditions & Long-Term Progression**: Aftercore state entry is not a complete repeatable Expedition/endgame loop.
- **14 — Collections, Codex, Museums, Discovery & Rare Finds**: Discovery projections exist; Codex, collections, museum and completion workflows open.
- **15 — Events, Holidays, Seasonal Content, World Events & FOMO Control**: Scheduler infrastructure does not establish an event framework or safe live cadence.
- **16 — Automation, UI, Parser, Information Architecture & Accessibility**: Folio, safe finite routines and explanations in fixtures; natural-language intent, equivalent surfaces and device accessibility gates open.
- **17 — Monetization, Supporter Tokens, IOTM Content & Pay-to-Win Boundaries**: Public funding copy is not payments, entitlements or supporter item fulfillment.
- **18 — World Structure, Regions, Travel, Maps, Time, Weather & World State**: Generic scoped state is not a region map, travel, weather or world resolver workflow.
- **19 — Home, Campground, Housing, Rest, Storage & Personal Infrastructure**: Storage/recovery primitives and Pack UI; complete Home infrastructure, upgrades and production open.
- **20 — Deities, Religion, Patrons, Omens, Constellations, Luck & Cosmology**: No integrated cosmology/deity/patron player workflow established.
- **21 — Factions, Standing, Politics, Law, Crime & Regional Allegiance**: No integrated factions, law/crime or regional allegiance workflow established.
- **22 — PvP, Duels, Arenas, Async Defense & Competitive Formats**: Shared combat is not PvP authorization, competitive format or fairness verification.

- **24 — Status Effects, Buffs, Debuffs, Diseases, Curses, Resistance, Cleansing & Effect Engine**: Typed tactical effects, cleansing, resistance and clocks; world/long-lived effect coverage open.
- **25 — Death, Defeat, Downed States, Resurrection, Retreat, Surrender & Recovery**: Tactical retreat, defeat and recovery; general resurrection, surrender and downed workflows open.
- **26 — Creature Level, Difficulty, Scaling, Enemy Progression & Encounter Challenge**: Authored encounter values/validation; full disclosed Creature-Level and scale contracts open.
